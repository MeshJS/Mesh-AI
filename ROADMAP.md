# Mesh AI Roadmap

---

Phase 1: 2025
Phase 1 focused on the ideation and initial establishment of use cases to support developers and ai-models/agents on their day to day coding work while building on Cardano. 

Outcomes:
- Mesh MCP Server
  An initial Model Context Protocol for AI Agents to get better input on the Mesh SDK and therefore being able to provide higher quality output
  
- RAG Pipeline
  A Retrieval-augmented generation based way to optimise developer documentation towards ai-optimised formats, making sure you Docs are as helpful to Devs as for AI models

---

Phase 2: 2026

Outcomes so far:

- Mesh SDK Skills for Claude Code
  Tailored skills for Claude Code, building upon the initial Mesh MCP and further enhancing AI models in the effective use of the Mesh SDK
- More to come, stay tuned

---

What Next ? 
We will always enhance and update our stack to the quickly evolving AI developer landscape, ensuring that our stack always follows trends, stays always optimised to new AI tooling and standards, and to ensure that Devlopers can use Mesh confidently and effectively with their AI Agentd and models.
Threfore, we dont even need a roadmap else than the objective to adapt and adopt our stack to the newest AI developer evolutions.
